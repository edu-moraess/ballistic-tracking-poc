## Interpretation caveat (mandatory)

> Validation used the **same image set** as training (`val = images/train`).  
> Consequently the metrics above measure performance on data that the model has already seen and **do not represent independent generalization performance**.

In addition, the labels themselves are pseudo-labels generated by background subtraction; they are not independent human or sensor ground truth.
